MASMS Privacy Policy

Maestro Advanced School Management System ("MASMS", "the app") is a school management tool used by schools, their staff, and the sales agents who register schools. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who is responsible

MASMS is operated by [YOUR COMPANY / DEVELOPER NAME]. For any privacy question or request, contact [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

Before publishing, replace the two bracketed items above with your real details.

Information the app handles

  • School records entered by the school: student and guardian details (names, admission numbers, phone numbers, photos), staff and teacher records, classes, timetables, attendance, examination marks, fees and payments, library, stores, boarding and transport records.
  • Account information: staff and sales-agent names, phone numbers, email addresses, role, and a salted, hashed password (we never store passwords in readable form).
  • Platform records kept by MASMS: the list of registered schools, sales agents, invoices and receipts, and sign-in activity (who signed in, when, and an approximate region) used for security.

How the information is used

Only to run the school's own features: showing records to authorised users, producing reports, invoices, receipts and printouts, and keeping the platform secure. We do not sell your information, and the app contains no advertising or third-party tracking.

Where it is stored

  • On your device: the app saves a copy of its data on the phone or computer so it works without internet.
  • In the cloud (when the cloud button is used): data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our database hosted on MongoDB Atlas, served through Vercel, so that authorised users can see the same data on different devices.

Who can see it

Only people the school (or MASMS) has given a login. Sales agents can see only the schools they registered. Parents and students can see only their own child's portal information. We share information with others only when the law requires it.

Children's information

The app stores information about students, who may be under 18. This information is entered and controlled by the school, which is responsible for having the right to collect it, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Students and parents should contact their school to see, correct or delete a record.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while a school uses MASMS. A school can ask us to delete its data by contacting the email above, and can export a full backup at any time from Settings > Backup & Restore. Uninstalling the app removes the copy stored on that device.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, connections use HTTPS, and access is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure, so schools should keep their passwords private and sign out on shared devices.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, or object to its use, by contacting the school or us at the email above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
