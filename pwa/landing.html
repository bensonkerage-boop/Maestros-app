Why Maestro

Less paperwork. More time for teaching.

School offices lose hours to spreadsheets, paper registers and chasing fees. Maestro Advanced School Management System keeps student records, attendance, finances and results in one place, so everyone works from the same up-to-date information.

Principals, registrars, bursars and teachers each see the tools they need, from the first enrolment of the year to the last report card.
